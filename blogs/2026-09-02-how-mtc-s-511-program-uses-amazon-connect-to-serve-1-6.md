Whether it’s checking when the next bus, train, or ferry departs, finding out about a freeway closure, or getting a broken-down car towed off the Bay Bridge, the region’s 511 traveler information service is a critical public resource. Its phone service runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS) using services Amazon Connect Customer and Amazon Lex.
