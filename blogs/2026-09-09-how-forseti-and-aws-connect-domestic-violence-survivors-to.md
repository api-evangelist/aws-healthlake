Learn how Forseti, a public safety technology company, built the Honest Assessment Response Tool (HART) to meet survivors in that moment. Running on Amazon Web Services (AWS), HART digitizes on-scene domestic violence risk screening, connects survivors to services in real time, and gives state agencies population-level visibility into intimate partner violence patterns.
