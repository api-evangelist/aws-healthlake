In less than 2 years, MiCloud, a statewide initiative with strategic support from Amazon Web Services (AWS), Michigan is building a resilient foundation for education technology that protects learning continuity for more than 568,000 students statewide, representing 42 percent of the state's 1.4-million student K12 population.
