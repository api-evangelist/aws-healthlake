Orygen, a globally recognized youth mental health research organization based in Melbourne, Australia, is exploring a different model. The organization is creating new ways for young people to engage with therapy from whatever device they have, wherever they are by combining immersive virtual environments with clinical practice and streaming them through Amazon Web Services (AWS).
