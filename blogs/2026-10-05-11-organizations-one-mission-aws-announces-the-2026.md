Today, Amazon Web Services (AWS) announces the recipients of the 2026 AWS Imagine Grant Children's Health Innovation Award (CHIA). Whether a child is battling a rare cancer, managing a chronic condition, or growing up downstream from a contaminated waterway, this year's 10 recipients share a common ambition: using technology to help every child get access to the best possible care.
