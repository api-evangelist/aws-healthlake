Colorado State University Global (CSU Global), a 100% online, accredited nonprofit university, set out to take on this opportunity by collaborating with Muzzy Lane, an educational technology company that supports instructional designers to design and deliver roleplay assessments directly in their learning management system (LMS). Muzzy Lane’s Author platform is powered by Amazon Bedrock, which supports its AI capabilities and protects customer data. By using Author, CSU Global designed its high-stakes roleplay assessments for its Bachelor of Science (BS) in Human Services program.
